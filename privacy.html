Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-11

Fruteria Dramatica Publisher ("the app") is a private tool run by the owner of the Frutería Dramática channel to publish that channel's own videos.

What the app accesses

  • YouTube: permission to upload videos to the owner's channel (scope youtube.upload).
  • Instagram and Facebook: permission to publish Reels to the owner's own Instagram account and Facebook Page.

How data is used

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's computer and are used only to upload the owner's videos. The app does not read, collect, store, sell or share personal data of any other person. It does not use analytics, cookies or advertising.

Google user data

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google account permissions or from Facebook's business integrations settings.
