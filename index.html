Fruteria Dramatica Publisher

Fruteria Dramatica Publisher is a private publishing tool used by the creator of the Frutería Dramática channel, an animated fruit telenovela series made with AI.

It uploads the channel's own episodes to the channel's own YouTube, Instagram and Facebook accounts on a set schedule. It is not offered to other users and does not access anyone else's account or data.
